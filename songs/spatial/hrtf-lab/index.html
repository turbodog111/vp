HRTF Clip Lab

Same 30 seconds of Doki Doki Forever (Traveling Voices Slow Glide map + phrase leads). Only the free HRTF ear filter changes. Use headphones.

Window: 46s–76s (Chorus 1) Clips: 8 Tip: pause one before starting the next for clean A/B
Don’t expect night-and-day differences on every clip. Pick the one that feels most outside your head on rear/side poses — that’s the fit for your ears. Dummy tags = measured on a mannequin; human tags = a real person (may fit you better by chance).
