BTS sources retrieved 2026-10-22. Their original AAC streams were remuxed
without re-encoding into fast-start M4A, preserving the selected uploads' timing.
These uploads are not substituted with album versions of different durations.

Motion data is generated by scripts/build_song_motion.py from those exact
M4A files. The JSON includes the recording SHA-256, measured duration,
25 Hz energy/bass/high/onset envelopes, estimated tempo and beat phase.
Envelopes use fixed recording-wide normalization, not per-frame leveling.
These are audio-driven visual dynamics, not a verified chorus or lyric map.
No lyric text or guessed singing timestamps have been added.

The renderer draws in VP only, under FX for supported songs. Desktop
wallpaper/theater registration is intentionally deferred. Native audio,
equalizer, queue ownership, and media clock remain the existing VP paths.
